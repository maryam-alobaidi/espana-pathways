Reunite Your Family in Barcelona

Under the Spanish immigration laws, legal residents can bring their family members (spouse, children, or parents) to live with them. Our law office, led by attorney Mireia at C/ Balmes 184, Barcelona, provides complete legal support with native Arabic-speaking guidance to ensure a smooth process.

  • Document preparation and legal eligibility check
  • Housing report assistance (Informe de Vivienda)
  • Full submission and follow-up at Extranjería
