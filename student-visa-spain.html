Spain Student Visa: Study, Live & Work

Unlock world-class education and a Mediterranean lifestyle. Discover the requirements and legal steps to study in Spain with expert guidance.

What is the Spain Student Visa?

The Spanish Student Visa is designed for non-EU nationals accepted into an authorized educational institution in Spain, such as universities, higher education academies, or accredited language schools.

  • ✅ Part-Time Work: Legally work up to 30 hours per week alongside your studies.
  • ✅ Path to Residency: Option to modify your status to a work or nomad permit later.
  • ✅ Family Integration: Bring your family members under specific conditions.

Key Requirements to Qualify

🏫 Acceptance Letter

Enrollment in an accredited Spanish school, university, or official training program.

💶 Financial Proof

Demonstrate sufficient funds (IPREM-based, usually around 600€+ per month for living expenses).

🏥 Health Insurance

Full private medical insurance coverage valid in Spain without copayments.

📋 Clear Record & Medical

Criminal record certificate (if staying over 6 months) and an official medical certificate.
