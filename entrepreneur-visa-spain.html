Spain Entrepreneur Visa: Launch Your Innovative Business

Bring your innovative startup or business project to Spain. Discover the requirements, approved business plans, and step-by-step process with expert legal guidance.

What is the Spain Entrepreneur Visa?

The Spanish Entrepreneur Visa is designed for non-EU nationals who wish to launch an innovative business project of special economic interest to Spain, evaluated based on your business plan and economic impact.

  • ✅ Duration: Valid for up to 3 years with clear renewal paths.
  • ✅ Family Friendly: Bring your spouse and children with you.
  • ✅ Ecosystem Access: Tap into Spain's thriving tech and business hubs.

Key Requirements to Qualify

💡 Innovative Business Plan

A detailed proposal demonstrating technological innovation, job creation, or socio-economic contribution to Spain.

📋 Official Approval

Favorable evaluation and certification of the business project from competent Spanish authorities (e.g., ENISA).

🎓 Professional Background

Proven expertise, relevant qualifications, and leadership capabilities to manage the proposed venture.

🏥 Health & Background

Clean criminal record check and private health insurance valid in Spain, plus sufficient financial means.
