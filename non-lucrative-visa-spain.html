Spain Non-Lucrative Visa: Retire & Live in Spain

Enjoy a peaceful life in Spain with financial independence. Discover the requirements, financial proofs, and application process with expert guidance.

What is the Non-Lucrative Visa?

The Spanish Non-Lucrative Residence Visa is tailored for non-EU citizens who want to reside in Spain without engaging in any work or professional activities. It is ideal for retirees or financially self-sufficient individuals.

  • ✅ Full Residency: Live in Spain for over 183 days a year with full legal status.
  • ✅ Family Inclusion: Easily include your spouse and dependent children in the application.
  • ✅ Path to Permanent Residency: Can lead to long-term residency after 5 years.

Key Requirements to Qualify

💶 Substantial Funds (IPREM)

Prove sufficient financial means (approx. 2,400€+ per month for the main applicant, plus extra for each family member).

🏥 Private Health Insurance

Full health insurance policy from an authorized provider in Spain with no co-payments.

📋 Clean Criminal Record

Certificate of no criminal convictions from your home country covering the past 5 years.

🩺 Medical Certificate

Official health certificate stating you do not suffer from any diseases that could quarantine public health.
